Why AuroraOSP

Built like a Pixel. Opened like AOSP.

Aurora starts from LineageOS 24.0 and layers a genuine Google software stack on top — then hands you the switches Google never shipped.

A real Pixel software stack

AuroraOSP ships Google's own Settings and System UI builds alongside Pixel framework overlays and partner modules — so Now Playing, Live Caption, adaptive charging and the Pixel launcher behave the way they do on a real Pixel, not an approximation of it.

SettingsGoogle SystemUIGoogle Pixel overlays Partner modules

GMS, Vanilla or Core

Three build flavours. The full Google stack, a minimal Core set, or a completely de-Googled Vanilla image — same Aurora feature set in each.

Passes Play Integrity

Built-in Play Integrity Fix with fetchable Pixel Beta fingerprints, TrickyStore keybox management, and per-app custom-ROM hiding.

Liquid Glass, everywhere it belongs

Aurora's own surface treatment — clear glass over the blurred backdrop, picking up your wallpaper — implemented across Quick Settings, the volume dialog, the power menu, the Settings homepage, the launcher and the lock screen clocks. Every part of it can be left on Default instead.

Quick Settings Volume dialog Power menu Settings homepage

Signature features

Things you won't find on stock.

Now Bar

Samsung's Now Bar, reverse-engineered from scratch, so live activities land on the lock screen of hardware that never shipped with One UI — including live sports scores and the real charge rate going into the device.

Lyrics on the lock screen

Aurora's music app looks lyrics up online and publishes them timed to the media session, so the lock screen follows the current line — as many lines as you want, and on the always-on display too.

Quick Settings, resized

Tiles resize to any width and height, each keeping its own size in the collapsed shade. A tabbed live editor applies your layout to the panel you're looking at, and QQS pages through every tile instead of truncating.

Eight power menus

Grid, capsule toolbar, size hierarchy, tonal outline, vertical list, bento grid, colour tiles and liquid glass — plus a live editor to rearrange the thing while you watch.

Depth and dimension

The lock screen clock is cut with your wallpaper's depth matte so the subject sits in front of it — rendered clock-side, so it survives style changes and the always-on display.

Attestation you can audit

TrickyStore checks an imported keybox against Google's revocation list and says plainly whether it is valid, revoked — with reason and serial — or unverifiable. Invalid keyboxes are refused, not silently installed.
