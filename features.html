Features

Every switch, documented.

AuroraOSP's customisation lives in Aurora Hub — one searchable Material 3 surface in Settings. Beyond it, Aurora reworks System UI, the launcher, the theme picker, files and music. Here is what all of it actually does.

Liquid Glass Now Bar Quick Settings Lockscreen Power menu Themes Status bar Apps Gestures & sound Security & spoofing

Liquid Glass

A translucent design language, applied consistently

Liquid Glass is Aurora's own surface treatment: clear glass over the blurred backdrop, picking up your wallpaper and matching your home screen icons. It isn't a single toggle bolted onto one screen — it's implemented across the system, and every part of it can be left on Default instead.

  • Quick Settings panel Glass tiles over the blurred backdrop instead of filled tiles on a solid panel, with glass-aware QS dialogs that stay legible on a narrow pane.
  • Volume dialog The volume slider redrawn as glass over a blurred backdrop, matching your Quick Settings, with its own blur intensity control.
  • Power menu Liquid glass is one of eight power menu styles — see below.
  • Settings homepage Four homepage layouts: stock Android, Aurora with rounded groups and a user card, Liquid Glass over your wallpaper, and Right Icons with icons aligned right. Plus an optional floating search bar pinned to the bottom of the screen rather than sitting in the header.
  • Launcher and theme picker The launcher options popup, the recents overview button, and the theme picker's custom clock styles all follow the glass treatment, with themed icons that stay themed under it.
  • Lock screen clocks and fingerprint Glass custom clock styles, and a glass treatment for the fingerprint affordance that drops its blur cleanly as the affordance fades out.

Now Bar

Live activities on the lock screen

  • Reverse-engineered from scratch Aurora implements Samsung's Now Bar independently, so live activities show up on the lock screen on hardware that never shipped with One UI.
  • Live sports Scores and game state pulled in and rendered in the bar, sized to fit rather than falling back to an empty flag square when a feed has no data.
  • Live charger input The actual charge rate going into the device shown on the lock screen while plugged in.
  • Theme styles The Now Bar has its own style options in Settings, so it can match the rest of your lock screen instead of imposing a look.

Quick Settings

Resizable tiles, a real layout editor, and blur

  • Resize any tile, any direction Tiles can be resized to any width and height — not just the two widths stock allows. Each tile keeps its own height, and a tall tile stays tall in the collapsed shade.
  • Tabbed live layout editor A tile editor with a dedicated layout tab that applies changes live to the panel you're actually looking at, rather than to a preview that drifts out of sync.
  • Page through every tile Quick quick settings pages by height through the full tile set instead of truncating at the first row, and settles cleanly when left between pages.
  • Blur customisation Control how much the screen behind is blurred when you pull down Quick Settings and notifications — independently from the launcher's drawer and recents blur.
  • Aurora tiles Performance mode and PC Mode ship as Quick Settings tiles.

Lockscreen

Lyrics, depth, edge light & always-on display

  • Live lyrics Follow along with the song on the lock screen. Aurora's music app looks the lyrics up online and publishes them timed to the media session, so System UI can track the current line — choose how many lines stay on screen, hold on the last line, and keep them up on the always-on display.
  • Depth effect The clock is cut with the wallpaper's depth matte so your subject sits in front of it, rendered on the clock side so it survives clock style changes and AOD.
  • Custom clock styles Custom clock styles with their own fonts, including Liquid Glass variants, applied live. Google's clock plugins are allowlisted, and the default clock is fully removed from the hierarchy when a style is active rather than hidden behind it.
  • Custom shortcut affordances Bind the lock screen shortcuts to any installed app through a custom app picker, not just the short stock list — with affordance styles in the theme picker.
  • Themeable fingerprint A themeable UDFPS icon and recognising animation, rendered through the device-entry icon and played via the auth ripple, with ultrasonic sensor support.
  • Edge Light Notification glow around the screen edges, coloured by the notification accent or a custom colour.
  • Media Art Album artwork on the lock screen during playback, with an adjustable blur level.
  • Pulse visualiser An audio visualiser on the lock screen and ambient display — bars, fading blocks or solid lines, with configurable bar count, rounded bars, and colour from lava lamp, album art or the system accent.
  • Always-On Display scheduling Always on, on a schedule, charge-only, or scheduled plus charging — with start and end times, a screen-off AOD duration, and an animated transition.

The glass treatment, on the power menu.

  • Live editor Rearrange the menu and see it change as you go. Emergency, Lockdown, Power off, Restart and Bug report are all first-class actions.

Themes & fonts

Icons, wallpapers and a real font engine

  • System fonts that actually change User-selected font overlays override the system fonts, including the variable-* families that normally ignore them. Activities relaunch on change so nothing is left half-styled, and the stock font keeps its name so Default genuinely restores it.
  • Icon packs and status bar icons Change app icons system-wide, and restyle the status bar Wi-Fi and signal icons — including a Wi-Fi standard indicator, a data-disabled icon, and a "show 4G instead of LTE" toggle. Icon overlays switch cleanly instead of stacking.
  • Live previews The theme picker previews icons live, previews the depth effect, and scrolls through clock styles — with a reset button on every slider.
  • Wallpapers Downloadable live wallpapers discovered and installed from inside the picker, a recent-wallpaper carousel, partner wallpapers read from every partner package, and curated photo effects.
  • Boot animation Import any bootanimation.zip with a live preview before applying, and reset to the stock Aurora animation in one tap.
  • Privacy indicator The camera and microphone privacy dot tinted with your accent colour, with stock icon shapes preserved.

Status bar

Size, padding and what shows up in it

  • Height and padding Make the whole status bar taller or shorter, and set start and end padding independently — useful for pushing content clear of a cutout. Zero keeps your screen's default.
  • Status bar tuner Show or hide individual status bar icons, including the battery preference.
  • Shade header actions The date, clock and battery in the shade header are clickable, each with its own destination.

Apps

Aurora reworks the ones you use daily

Music

Aurora's music player finds what your library is missing: it looks up lyrics online and publishes them timed to the session for the lock screen, fetches missing cover art, and finds artist and genre artwork — for one album or the whole library at once. Artwork found online is cached and can be forgotten again.

Files

A real bin you can browse and restore from, Safe folder support, starred files, and a Material 3 accent pill floating bar with Quick Share and the document scanner. Opens on Recents, handles rotation, and knows when a provider refuses to trash something.

Launcher

Independent blur intensity for the app drawer and recents, themed icons for suggested apps and recents tasks, live calendar and clock dynamic icons, a tightened bottom search bar, and smartspace cards that fall back to options when a tap can't land.

Settings

Four homepage styles, a floating search bar, the profile icon moved beside the title, a font picker whose default means default, and a rounded maximum battery capacity — with a device-side override for a misreported design capacity.

  • Pixel stack, current Google's Settings and System UI builds tracked to Android 17, with the Gemini live screen share refiner, Now Playing, the dream weather complication, CHRE activity recognition, contextual cursor, screen capture and the Columbus tap gesture (with a real sensitivity slider) all wired into the graph.
  • USB prompt Plugging in a cable raises a proper bottom sheet for choosing the USB mode instead of burying it in a notification.

Gestures, sound & essentials

The everyday switches

  • Shake and three-finger gestures Shake to trigger an action with a sensitivity threshold, plus separate three-finger swipe and long-press actions — bindable to 16 targets including app switch, voice search, camera, last app, flashlight, screenshot, volume panel, clear notifications and ringer mode cycling.
  • Disable button screenshot Turn off the power + volume-down combo when it keeps firing by accident.
  • Per-app volume & multi audio focus An independent, remembered volume level per app, and the option to let several apps play audio at once instead of one stealing focus.
  • Essential notifications Nominate the apps that matter and Aurora keeps them grouped at the top of the shade. Media resume controls expire faster and are dropped on reboot.
  • GameSpace & Sidebar A gaming overlay with per-game handling, and an edge-swipe sidebar for quick access to favourite apps over whatever is on screen.

Security & spoofing

Play Integrity, attestation & per-app properties

  • Play Integrity Fix Build fingerprint spoofing with a built-in device preset as a fallback, a fetcher that pulls the latest or canary Pixel Beta fingerprint on demand, and a documented priority chain (custom.pif.* beats pif.*) so your own config always wins. first_api_level is spoofed at the property layer in both Java and native, and build fields can be spoofed reflectively.
  • TrickyStore with revocation checking Keybox management for key attestation that actually validates what you import: Aurora checks the keybox against Google's revocation list and tells you plainly whether it is valid, revoked (with the reason and serial), or unverifiable because Google couldn't be reached. Invalid or expired keyboxes are refused rather than silently installed. EC keyboxes load as Conscrypt-usable private keys, and the leaf-hack path emits attestation tags in the right order.
  • Per-app spoofing Present different device properties to individual apps, with built-in profiles for ROG Phone 8 Pro, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Xiaomi 13 Pro, OnePlus 9 Pro, Black Shark 4 and Lenovo Y700 — plus custom key/value presets you can save and reuse. Backed by a system-level app spoof service rather than per-app hooks.
  • Attestation targeting Apply attestation spoofing system-wide, or pick apps individually and import a target list from a file. Auto, leaf-hack and certificate-generation modes per target.
  • Google Photos spoofing Present as a supported Pixel to Google Photos for unlimited original-quality backup.
  • Ignore window secure Allow screenshots and screen recording in apps that block them, when you need to.
